## How I work

I build to solve a problem, follow a curiosity, or make something I wish existed. The projects here show the results of that work.

1. **Understand the workflow.** Map the people, decisions, constraints, and places work gets stuck.
2. **Build the whole path.** Connect the interface, data, and integrations around shared rules.
3. **Make it dependable.** Test real behavior, make failures visible, and build in recovery.

I stay close to the product, the code, and the people who use it—from architecture and implementation through release and maintenance. I use AI as part of that workflow, with changes reviewed and behavior verified before I call the work done.
